SPECTRAM 2027 / CONFERENCE

About

Purpose, scope and research opportunities across the SPECTRAM 2027 technical community.

01 / ABOUT

Connecting ideas across RF & wireless technology.

The conference aims to bring together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, scientists, and students working in the fields of microwave engineering, antenna design, electromagnetic wave propagation, wireless communications, RF systems, and emerging signal processing technologies.

The conference will serve as a platform for presenting cutting-edge research, exchanging technical knowledge, fostering collaborations, and discussing future trends in antenna and propagation technologies.

02 / CONFERENCE FOCUS

Objectives & Submission Guidelines

Research presentation, collaboration, IEEE professional activities and emerging technology areas.

Research Forum

To provide an international forum for presenting innovative research in microwaves, antennas, wireless and Signal Processing.

Collaboration

To facilitate collaboration between academia, research laboratories, and industry.

IEEE Activities

To promote IEEE professional activities and membership.

Emerging Technologies

To explore the next generation of signal processing, wireless communication, antenna, microwave, and RF technologies

Submission Guidelines

The following submission guidelines apply

01 Authors are invited to submit original, unpublished, and high-quality research papers presenting significant theoretical, methodological, experimental, or application-oriented contributions.
02 All accepted, registered, and presented papers that comply with IEEE’s required quality, technical, and formatting standards will be submitted to IEEE for consideration for inclusion in the IEEE Xplore® Digital Library.
03 At least one author must register and present each accepted paper at the conference for the paper to be considered for inclusion in the conference proceedings and submission to IEEE Xplore, subject to applicable IEEE policies and requirements.
