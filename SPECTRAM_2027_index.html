01 / ABOUT

Connecting ideas across RF & wireless technology.

The conference aims to provide a platform for presenting cutting-edge research, exchanging technical knowledge, fostering collaborations, and discussing future trends in antenna and propagation technologies.

It brings together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, scientists and students working in microwave engineering, antenna design, electromagnetic wave propagation, wireless communications, RF systems and emerging signal processing technologies.
