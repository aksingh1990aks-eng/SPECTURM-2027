01 / ABOUT

Connecting ideas across RF & wireless technology.

The conference aims to bring together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, scientists, and students working in the fields of microwave engineering, antenna design, electromagnetic wave propagation, wireless communications, RF systems, and emerging signal processing technologies.

The conference will serve as a platform for presenting cutting-edge research, exchanging technical knowledge, fostering collaborations, and discussing future trends in antenna and propagation technologies.

02 / CONFERENCE FOCUS

Objectives & Expected Outcomes

Research presentation, collaboration, IEEE professional activities and emerging technology areas.

Research Forum

To provide an international forum for presenting innovative research in microwaves, antennas, wireless and Signal Processing.

Collaboration

To facilitate collaboration between academia, research laboratories, and industry.

IEEE Activities

To promote IEEE professional activities and membership.

Emerging Technologies

To encourage research in emerging 5G/6G, IoT, AI-enabled RF systems, and satellite communications.

EXPECTED OUTCOMES

What the event aims to deliver

01 High-quality peer-reviewed conference proceedings.
02 Publication of accepted papers in IEEE Xplore (subject to IEEE approval).
03 Increased collaboration among researchers and industry experts.
04 Promotion of advanced wireless communication technologies and antenna research.
