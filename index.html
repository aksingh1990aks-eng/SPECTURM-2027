SPECTRAM 2027

2027 International Conference on Signal Processing, Electromagnetics, Communication Technologies , RF Antenna and Microwaves

Beyond the Visible Spectrum

The electromagnetic spectrum is the invisible backbone of modern civilization. SPECTRAM'27 converges in GGV, Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh — at the intersection of tradition and innovation — to explore the next generation of signal processing, wireless communication, antenna, microwave, and RF technologies. From cognitive radio to reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, from metamaterials to spectrum sharing, this is where the future of electromagnetic engineering takes shape.

Explore Conference Technical Tracks
IEEE SPECTRAM’27 welcomes original research contributions from academia, industry, and R&D communities addressing emerging challenges, innovative solutions, and technological advancements across the following technical tracks. The conference provides a platform for researchers and practitioners to exchange ideas, foster interdisciplinary collaboration, and advance innovative technologies toward future-ready applications.

Welcome to SPECTRAM 2027

01 / ABOUT

Connecting ideas across RF & wireless technology.

The conference aims to bring together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, scientists, and students working in the fields of microwave engineering, antenna design, electromagnetic wave propagation, wireless communications, RF systems, and emerging signal processing technologies.

The conference will serve as a platform for presenting cutting-edge research, exchanging technical knowledge, fostering collaborations, and discussing future trends in antenna and propagation technologies.

Registration Guidelines

  • One author registration covers one paper and one presenting author only. Authors submitting multiple papers must complete a separate registration for each paper, as per the applicable registration fee structure.
  • If more than one co-author wishes to attend the conference, an additional attendee registration fee will be applicable for each additional participant.
  • The standard paper length is six (6) pages. Each additional page beyond the six-page limit will incur an extra charge of USD 15 per page for international authors and INR 500 per page for Indian authors.
  • All accepted, registered, and presented papers that comply with IEEE’s required quality, technical, and formatting standards will be submitted to IEEE for consideration for inclusion in the IEEE Xplore® Digital Library.
  • At least one author must register and present each accepted paper at the conference for the paper to be considered for inclusion in the conference proceedings and submission to IEEE Xplore, subject to applicable IEEE policies and requirements.
