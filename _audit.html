Beyond the Visible Spectrum

The electromagnetic spectrum is the invisible backbone of modern civilization. SPECTRAM'27 converges in GGV, Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh — at the intersection of tradition and innovation — to explore the next generation of signal processing, wireless communication, antenna, microwave, and RF technologies. From cognitive radio to reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, from metamaterials to spectrum sharing, this is where the future of electromagnetic engineering takes shape.

◷ 21–23 August 2027 ⌖ Guru Ghasidas Vishwavidyalaya, Bilaspur, India

DISCOVER SPECTRAM’27

A forum for ideas across the electromagnetic spectrum.

SPECTRAM 2027 brings together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, scientists and students working in signal processing, electromagnetics, communication technologies, RF, antennas and microwave engineering.

The conference aims to support technical exchange, research presentation and collaboration across established and emerging technologies.
