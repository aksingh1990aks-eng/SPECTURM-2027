Paper Submission Guidelines

Please read the following instructions and policies carefully before submitting your manuscript.

Originality & Scope

Manuscripts must present original, unpublished research contributing significantly to the conference tracks. Submissions must not be currently under consideration for publication elsewhere.

Format & Page Limits

Papers must follow the standard IEEE double-column format (A4 size). The standard page limit is six (6) pages including all figures, tables, and references. Over-length page charges apply up to a maximum limit.

Plagiarism & Similarity

Papers will be screened using CrossCheck / Turnitin. Submissions with high similarity scores will be desk-rejected. Authors must comply strictly with the IEEE Plagiarism Guidelines.

Peer Review Policy

All submissions undergo rigorous, double-blind peer review by at least two domain experts. Review criteria focus on technical quality, significance, novelty, clarity, and relevance to the conference scope.

Key Author Requirements & Compliance

  • Submission Mode: All manuscripts must be submitted electronically in PDF format via the designated conference portal. Direct submissions through email will not be accepted.
  • Blind Submission: Do not include author names, affiliations, email addresses, or acknowledgment acknowledgments in the initial submission draft to facilitate blind review.
  • Camera-Ready Verification: Final accepted papers must pass IEEE PDF eXpress compliance checks before camera-ready submission.
  • Copyright Transfer: Authors of accepted papers must transfer publication rights to IEEE via the IEEE Electronic Copyright (eCF) system prior to publication.
  • Presentation Policy (No-Show Rule): At least one author must register at the non-student/appropriate author rate and present the paper at the conference. Papers that are not presented will be excluded from submission to IEEE Xplore.

Submission Workflow & Resources

  1. Download Template: Format your paper strictly using the official templates from IEEE Conference Publishing.
  2. Review Ethics & Guidelines: Verify compliance with the IEEE Plagiarism Guidelines.
  3. Submit PDF: Submit your blinded PDF through the online conference submission portal before the deadline.
For any issues related to submission, authors may contact the organizing committee at ieeespectramggv@gmail.com
